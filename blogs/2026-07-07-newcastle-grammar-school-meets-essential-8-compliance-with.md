Newcastle Grammar School closed a long-standing MFA gap for students without personal devices by deploying Clever's visual authentication integrated with Microsoft Entra ID, reducing SOC security alerts and unlocking modern authentication features like Windows Hello and Face ID. The school can now confidently answer board and cyber insurance questions on MFA coverage, with plans to extend the program to Years 8–9 and beyond.
