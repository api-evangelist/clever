Canada's largest school board needed a smarter, more secure way to manage student data across a growing suite of learning applications—without adding to its IT team's workload. With Clever, Toronto District School Board built a customized, application-level rostering model that automated data sync from PowerSchool, gave teachers class-level access, and strengthened data security for 500,000+ students and staff.
