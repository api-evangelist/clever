Vita Multi Academy Trust deployed Clever's Classroom MFA, a multi-factor authentication solution usable by pupils as young as Year 3 without disrupting classroom operations. The trust achieved Cyber Essentials compliance ahead of the April 2026 deadline while reducing IT support tickets, enabling over 1,250 pupils and staff to authenticate securely through eight different methods.
