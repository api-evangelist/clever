Cornerstone C of E Primary School replaced shared login credentials with unique, secure passwords for every student using Clever's single sign-on platform and Clever Badges. The implementation made classroom and home learning accessible across Reception through Year 6 while eliminating security vulnerabilities tied to shared device credentials.
